## 2. Format mix
- **Festival/"విశిష్టత" Q&A clips**: 1–5 min clips from a studio interview (presenter asks, scholar answers), sharing one Karthika Masam studio set. Right now this means about 15 Karthika clips per day (deepam, dānam, nadi snānam, upavāsam, Nagula Chavithi, Ekadashi).
- **Pravachanam episodes**: 15–30 min segments from named speakers (Chaganti, Srisaila series). They drove the 2016–2019 views.
- **Dharma Sandehalu**: 2–6 min viewer-letter Q&A on household rules (Dr. Machiraju Venugopal). Six of the top 30 videos ever come from this series.
- **Stotram/song**: 4–30 min. These are the all-time top videos (126M, 32M, 13M, 10M) and newer singer-led songs (8M, 5.8M).
- **Astrology**: daily Kalachakram (<1 min), weekly rasi phalalu (one ~1.5 min clip per rasi plus a 23-min full show). This is now their **fastest-growing recent format**.
- **Devotional news / events**: temple updates, Koti Deepotsavam promos (2026 edition starts **14 Nov**), and 30-min event segments.
- **Shorts**: song clips, temple moments, viral emotional clips. Top Shorts reach 2–10M views.

Duration mix of the last 150 videos: 36% under 2 min, 23% 2–5 min, 25% 5–15 min, 13% 15–60 min, 3% over 60 min. Median 3:25.

## 3. Title patterns (as formulas)
- **A. Question + promise (current style):** `<context: month/festival> <question>? <promise: పుణ్యఫలితాలు/విశిష్టత> ఇవే! | <English tag> | Bhakthi TV`. Titles run 70–100 characters and are mostly Telugu, with an English keyword segment for search.
- **B. Conditional prosperity hook (Dharma Sandehalu era):** `<household object> <place/direction> ఉంటే <ఐశ్వర్యం/result>`. Short (under 40 Telugu characters), with no question mark.
- **C. Curiosity hook:** `… ఎందుకు …? / … మీకు తెలుసా? / … రహస్యం / అసలు కథ ఏమిటి?`
- **D. Series and speaker tags:** `|| Sri Chaganti Koteswara Rao || Bhakthi TV` (older videos use `||`, newer ones a single `|`). The speaker name goes in the title, and the full credit goes in the description line 1.
- **E. Date-stamped:** `<show> | 03rd November 2026 | Daily Horoscope | Bhakthi TV` and `2026 నవంబర్ నెలలో…`.
- Emojis are rare (🚩 on songs, 🔥 on Shorts). Every description carries 4–6 hashtags (#KarthikaMasam #NagaPooja …). The brand suffix is always last.

## 4. Thumbnail design
- **Text:** 2–3 lines of very heavy, rounded-terminal display Telugu filling 60–70% of the frame. Colours are **yellow + white on dark red/maroon or black**, with occasional green/orange accent words. A thick dark stroke or drop shadow sits behind the text. Usually 4–8 words, the hook phrase rather than the full title.
- **Imagery:** speaker cut-out on the right (pointing or teaching gesture), a deity or ritual object as background, and warm lamp tones. Stotram thumbnails use full-frame deity art with an ornate serif Telugu title.
- **Branding:** a small red-and-gold "భక్తిTV" bug at top-right (sometimes mid-right). Current clips add a **paid sponsor band** along the bottom (a matrimony ad) plus a templated show plate ("కార్తికమాసం / సందేహాలు–సమాధానాలు").
- **Our implication:** keep the bold yellow/white-on-maroon readability. Use deity art or diyas in place of a speaker face, and keep the bottom-right clear for YouTube's timestamp.

## 5. Script style (from transcripts)
Sources are YouTube Telugu auto-captions (ASR) for three videos: Dharma Sandehalu "broom direction" (5:51), the Nagula Chavithi pooja clip (3:24), and the Ekadashi vratam/Ambarisha katha (12:19). The Chaganti pravachanam has **no captions**, so its style is not verified.

**Style DNA**
1. **Open (0–15 s):** no greeting or intro music in the clips. The video begins straight into the question ("మరో ఉత్తరం చూద్దాం…", or the presenter's question), followed by a one-line framing of why the day or object matters.
2. **Register:** spoken, warm Telugu with grantha words embedded (శాస్త్రం, అంతరార్థం, విశిష్టత, నక్తం, ఏకభుక్తం). Heavy fillers: "అండి", "అన్నమాట", "కదా", "అనేటువంటి", "అని చెప్పేసి".
3. **Sentences:** long, chained clauses, with very few full stops in the ASR.
4. **Sanskrit terms:** the term is explained in place as term + plain gloss (e.g. naktam = eat only at night; ekabhuktam = one meal). Tithi positions are counted out (పాడ్యమి, విదియ, తదియ, చవితి).
5. **Slokas:** rare in Q&A clips. A short mantra/nama is offered as a practice ("…నమః అనుకుంటూ"). Katha videos retell a Purana story (Ambarisha–Durvasa) instead of quoting it.
6. **Phala-shruti:** frequent, often with symbolic rationale (anthill = womb), and absolute ("అదృష్టం కలుగుతుంది", "ఐశ్వర్యం", "సంతానం"). Do/don't directions are given as rules (ఈశాన్యంలో చీపురు నిషిద్ధం).
7. **CTA:** soft and in-voice, e.g. listen to the channel's morning bhakti songs. No subscribe asks in the clips.
8. **Close:** the clip ends mid-thought or with a summary moral. There is no formal sign-off.
9. **Pace:** **~101–108 ASR words/min**. Telugu words are agglutinative, so the speech feels moderate.

## 6. Gaps and opportunities for a faceless, rights-clean channel
- **Diaspora/NRI timing:** Bhakthi TV assumes IST and AP/Telangana panchangam. Fill this with "what date and time in CH/UK/US" tithi and muhurtham guides, which no one gives per time zone.
- **Discoverability:** their 30/day TV clips are untagged by depth and duplicated. Answer one question per video with chapters, sources (which Purana/Shastra), and a written summary in the description.
- **Measured phala-shruti:** they state results as certainties. We can cite the source text and frame results as tradition ("శాస్త్రం చెబుతోంది"), which is safer for trust and for YouTube policy.
- **Month-ahead calendars:** "festivals of the month" got 9.1K views in 1 day, beating their festival clips. Publish ours 7–10 days ahead with NRI dates.
- **Evergreen stotram with meaning:** their top views are bare audio. Add a line-by-line Telugu meaning with on-screen text in Suranna/Noto Serif, using our own or public-domain recitations only. Never reuse their audio, art or speaker footage.
- **Shorts:** their Shorts are clips of TV footage. Ours can be 30–45 s original explainers (one ritual = one Short) with bold Ramabhadra captions.

**Not verified:** 14-day view totals and subscriber trend (Social Blade unreachable); the pravachanam transcript style (no captions); live-stream viewership.
