## Who I am talking to

One person: a Telugu-speaking householder, 35–60, in Hyderabad, Vijayawada or abroad (USA, UK, Gulf),
who keeps the festivals at home but no longer has an elder nearby to ask "what exactly do we do today,
and why?". They have five minutes before the evening deepam, they trust the tradition, and they are
tired of videos that shout, threaten bad luck or promise riches.

## Format

- Faceless. No presenter on camera. Original painted-style devotional art, slow camera movement,
  Telugu text on screen for every key instruction, date or sloka.
- Human Telugu voiceover (calm, warm, unhurried, ~120–135 words a minute). A synthetic voice is used
  only for drafts and is replaced before upload.
- Long videos 5–9 minutes. Shorts 30–55 seconds, vertical, cut from the long video.
- Opens straight on the day and the question; no channel intro, no "like, share, subscribe" at the start.
- Background: tanpura drone, very low; silence under any mantra or sloka.
- One ask at the end: "ఈ రోజు దీపం వెలిగించారా? కామెంట్‌లో చెప్పండి" or the next video.

## How I actually talk

(To fill from recordings.) Target register: పుస్తక భాష కాదు, ఇంట్లో పెద్దవారు చెప్పే తెలుగు —

Address the viewer as "మీరు". Short sentences. Dates said with weekday ("మంగళవారం, నవంబర్ 10").

## Words I never use

- Fear and greed hooks: "ఇది చేయకపోతే అరిష్టం", "తప్పక కోటీశ్వరులు అవుతారు", "100% ఫలితం", "షాకింగ్".
- English filler: "guys", "let's dive in", "game-changer", "unlock".
- Claims of miracles or guaranteed results from any puja, vratam or mantra.

## Words I do use

- "సంప్రదాయం ప్రకారం", "పెద్దలు చెప్పినట్లు", "మీ కుటుంబ ఆచారం ప్రకారం చేయండి"
- "ఈ రోజు విశేషం", "ఎందుకంటే…", "చిన్న పని – పెద్ద భావం"
- Closing line: "శుభం భూయాత్."

## What I will not claim

- No date that has not been checked against a printed Telugu panchangam; where Smarta/Vaishnava
  or regional practice differs, say both dates.
- No phala-shruti presented as a promise; say "పురాణం చెప్పిన ఫలం" and attribute it.
- No mantra taught for chanting that needs initiation; Vedic mantras only as shravanam by trained pandits.
- No borrowed audio, label recordings or other channels' art. All images original.
- No medical, astrological-remedy or financial advice.
