| **Total** | **≈ 2.4 h** | **≈ 0.9 h** | **≈ 0.3 h** |

≈ 3.6 hours a week. **If the week goes wrong, drop the cheap one first**, then the third Short. Never drop the anchor in Karthika weeks.

## Running costs to know

- Images: each painting costs 70 Gamma credits; the account had 330 and now has 50. Four paintings per anchor
  means a Gamma plan top-up, or switching to Canva image generation (needs a design export step), before week 2.
- Voice: record in Telugu yourself or with a ₹20,000-cap helper; synthetic narration on faceless videos is the
  combination YouTube's "inauthentic content" policy targets.
